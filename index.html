  • Stock Watchlist

    Tracks the stocks, ETFs and funds you're watching, and the ones you already hold across the family's portfolios. Set a target buy price and an alert level and rows light up when a stock enters the buy zone or crosses above it, with a macOS notification. Log purchase lots to see average cost, current value and gain; click any row for a price chart, fundamentals and recent news. Live data from Yahoo Finance, converted to your currency. Runs locally on your Mac and stores everything in a single file you own.

  • Millionærklubben Transcript & Panel Sentiment

    Turns each episode of the Danish investing podcast Millionærklubben into data. Pulls the episode, transcribes it locally with Whisper on Apple Silicon, has Claude pick out the companies discussed, and appends tickers, panel sentiment and reported price moves to an Excel workbook. Run it from the terminal or a small local web UI that streams the log, shows the latest results, helps file mis-heard company names and follows your Mac's light or dark appearance. Scores are read as bands, not decimals. A personal research tool, not investment advice.
